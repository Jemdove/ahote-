Story behind ahoteɛ

One person, a mop, and
a big dream.

ahoteɛ didn't start in a boardroom. It started with one person who looked at dirty spaces and thought; I can fix that. Armed with nothing but determination, a mop, and an obsession with making things spotless, ahoteɛ was born in Accra.

Now, that one person has a full crew of proud, trained professionals serving homes, offices, and events across the Ghana. We've mopped hundreds of floors, scrubbed countless kitchens, and left behind the kind of clean that actually makes people smile.

The name ahoteɛ means "cleanliness" in Twi, and that's exactly what we give you. Cleanliness from the mess, clean space and, envonriment to enjoy your space.

Local hands. Global standards.

Our cleaners are trained individuals; people who take pride in the work and the homes they care for.
